The Miro Dynamics - HKU CGVU Joint Lab is a research collaboration between [Miro Dynamics](https://mirod.ai/) and the [CGVU Lab](/) at the University of Hong Kong. We work toward a human-native world model for embodied AI: we capture everyday human activity with egocentric, multimodal sensing, reconstruct human motion and interaction in 3D, and learn models of human behavior that robots then build on.
